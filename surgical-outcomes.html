FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When is surgery necessary for a fracture?

Surgical intervention is recommended when a fracture is displaced, unstable, involves a joint surface, or cannot be adequately aligned through conservative management. The objective of surgery is to restore anatomical alignment, ensure stable fixation, and allow early mobilization to achieve optimal functional recovery.

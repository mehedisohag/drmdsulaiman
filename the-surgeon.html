Dr Md Sulaiman is an Orthopedic & Trauma Surgeon in Brahmanbaria and Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a clinical focus on Spine Surgery, Complex Trauma, Ilizarov Surgery, Limb Lengthening, Deformity Correction, and Limb Reconstruction.

He graduated from Mymensingh Medical College (MBBS, 2013), completed his Diploma in Orthopedics (2017), and passed MRCS Part A (UK, 2023). He is currently pursuing FCPS Advanced Training in Orthopedics at Shaheed Suhrawardy Medical College & Hospital, Dhaka.

An active orthopedic researcher with international publications, he is committed to evidence-based spine and trauma care, ethical surgical practice, and patient-centered treatment.
